# Korean calibration dataset — annotation guideline

Status: planning only. Ratings describe the sound of one recording, not the speaker.

## Rater workflow

- Hear one randomized recording at a time with anonymous IDs.
- Use headphones where possible; complete a short hearing/device check.
- Rate each target dimension on a bounded ordered scale with an explicit “cannot judge” option.
- Do not infer age, identity, occupation, attractiveness, personality, truthfulness, health, biological sex, or gender identity.
- Do not rate technical defects as voice attributes. Flag severe noise, clipping, unintelligibility, or playback failures separately.

Each utterance needs at least 8 independent raters. Randomize order and include repeated hidden items to estimate intra-rater consistency. Insert licensed anchor samples only after their rights and consent scopes are verified; anchors align scale use, not a "correct" identity category.

## Dimension instructions

Use concrete auditory anchors: e.g., brightness concerns perceived high-frequency resonance, softness concerns onset/airflow impression, and pitch stability concerns short-term consistency in this recording. Give neither a preferred direction nor a gendered ideal. Preserve each rater value; never replace individual ratings with a mean before reliability analysis.

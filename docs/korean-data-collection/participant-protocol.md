# Korean calibration dataset — participant protocol

Status: planning only.

1. Read the study summary and choose consent scopes independently.
2. Confirm adult eligibility; receive a random participant ID.
3. Choose mobile or desktop recording. Record browser/OS class, microphone route, and coarse environment category only; do not collect unnecessary device identifiers.
4. Read two standard Korean passages at a comfortable natural voice, then provide one free utterance. Each recording is checked locally for clipping, duration, and speech presence.
5. Recommended minimum: 30 seconds per passage; do not prompt participants to strain, lower, raise, or gender their voice.
6. Allow retry, skip, pause, and withdrawal at every step. Do not expose ratings or inferred attributes to participants as feedback during collection.

Standard passages must cover diverse Korean vowels, plosives, fricatives, affricates, liquids, nasals, 받침, syllable boundaries, numbers, and varied prosody. The final script set needs native-speaker review and a pilot intelligibility check before collection.

No researcher or rater sees participant identity, consent choices beyond required access control, or contact details while rating.
